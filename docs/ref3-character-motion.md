# Reference short: CHARACTER MOTION notes (frame by frame, 30 fps, 540x960 sheets, 6 frames each)
Why this file exists: the first pass (docs/ref3-frames-detail.md) described the soldier as "stands / crouches". The real animation is MUCH richer: he acts out every beat (digs with a scraping arm, stands up, ducks when the wave comes, is lifted by the water, treads water...). Our demo had a standing mannequin. Rule for our videos: the hero is ALWAYS doing something that matches the sentence, and reacts to every threat with a visible anticipation -> impact -> recovery.
Character: stylised 3D soldier (olive Brodie helmet, navy tee, grey shorts, brown belt, black boots), smooth shading, no face detail (helmet brim hides eyes), always seen in side/3-quarter profile when in the cut-away hole.

- Take-away rules: (1) every threat gets ~3 frames of anticipation, ~3 frames of impact reaction, 6+ frames of held struggle; (2) water/foam = stretched white spheres, not a fluid sim; (3) the camera never cuts, it pushes and tilts during the reaction; (4) the hero is never idle for more than ~10 frames.
