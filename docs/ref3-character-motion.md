# Reference short: CHARACTER MOTION notes (frame by frame, 30 fps, 540x960 sheets, 6 frames each)
Why this file exists: the first pass (docs/ref3-frames-detail.md) described the soldier as "stands / crouches". The real animation is MUCH richer: he acts out every beat (digs with a scraping arm, stands up, ducks when the wave comes, is lifted by the water, treads water...). Our demo had a standing mannequin. Rule for our videos: the hero is ALWAYS doing something that matches the sentence, and reacts to every threat with a visible anticipation -> impact -> recovery.
Character: stylised 3D soldier (olive Brodie helmet, navy tee, grey shorts, brown belt, black boots), smooth shading, no face detail (helmet brim hides eyes), always seen in side/3-quarter profile when in the cut-away hole.

## Frames 96-143 (A FOXHOLE -> BUT THE -> FIRST WAVE -> FILLS IT)
- 96-101 DIG LOOP: half-crouch against the hole wall, knees bent ~45 deg, torso leaning forward into the wall, helmet down. The near arm scrapes the wall: hand touches the wall at 96, is pulled out at 97-98, back to the wall at 99, out at 100-101 = a ~3-frame scoop period (10 Hz), tiny sand puffs at the wall. Boots planted, body does not move otherwise.
- 102-104 STAND UP (3 frames, fast): knees straighten, torso rises, the arm swings back down behind his hip (102 hand at wall, 103 arm mid-air, 104 arm hanging). Ease-out, no overshoot.
- 104-113 IDLE LOOK (10 frames): standing straight, feet together on the hole floor, near arm hanging a little forward with a relaxed elbow, helmet slightly tipped UP at 105-107 (he looks at the incoming wave), then still. Only a 1-2 px sway. A wave builds on the horizon (rolling crest 102-107), a translucent teal wedge (the water volume) slides in from the left at 108-114 growing taller.
- 114-115 ANTICIPATION: the wave crest reaches the rim; he lifts the near hand toward the wall/chest (115) and his torso starts to turn away.
- 116-119 DUCK (4 frames): the wave mesh covers his head; he folds down - torso hunches forward, knees bend, helmet tips forward, arms come up over the head (118-119). White foam blobs (stretched white spheres) spawn on the crest.
- 120-125 BRACED HOLD (6 frames): crouched, back curved, helmet down, one hand sticks up ABOVE the water line and waves slowly (visible at 120-125 as a pale hand in the teal). Water cut line sits at his head height. Camera starts a slow crane UP + push in (hole gets ~1.4x bigger by 143, rim moves down the frame).
- 126-128 BLAST: white elongated spray particles (ellipsoids stretched along velocity) burst up from the hole (50+ of them), 126 first, 127-128 peak; the helmet pops up at 127, he unfolds.
- 129-134 PUSHED UP: head back looking up-left (129), both arms thrown UP into a V (130-131), body fully extended on tiptoe (131-134). The water in the hole starts to appear as a convex-topped translucent volume from the floor (131).
- 133-135 FLOAT-OFF: a boot lifts at 133, only toes touch at 134, feet leave the floor at 135 (he is now buoyant). Water level inside rises slowly 135-143 (curved top, darker blue side walls).
- 135-143 TREADING: helmet seen from behind (he turned his back to us), both arms flap alternately (one up and waving, the other reaching sideways to the wall), shoulders rock, chest-deep in the water; spray particles keep falling (150+ white spheres/ellipsoids in the air). Caption FILLS IT starts at 135.
- Take-away rules: (1) every threat gets ~3 frames of anticipation, ~3 frames of impact reaction, 6+ frames of held struggle; (2) water/foam = stretched white spheres, not a fluid sim; (3) the camera never cuts, it pushes and tilts during the reaction; (4) the hero is never idle for more than ~10 frames.

## Frames 0-47 (c001-c008)  [added out of order; read before 96-143]

## Frames 48-71 (c009-c012): JUMP INTO THE HOLE
- f48-51: cover pose held (hands on helmet, back hunched). White spike (shell) grows bigger each frame while falling toward the ship (scale up = coming at camera). Orange flash dot on ship stays.
- f51-52: ANTICIPATION: the near arm/elbow lifts higher (hands rise ABOVE helmet), knees start to bend, body gets taller (stretch up) -> jump launch at f52.
- f52-53: LAUNCH. Feet leave the ground; boots point down, knees tuck forward (thighs horizontal-ish), hands stay raised over head in a "diver" pose. He rises ~1 body-width in 2 frames.
- f54-59: AIRBORNE hang, knees bent ~90 deg, torso upright, arms up over helmet, slightly leaning back; slowly drifting LEFT toward the pit (~5 px/frame in sheet scale) and DOWN. Shell explodes at f55-60 on the far side: orange fireball + tan/black sphere clusters (low-poly puffs, 3 colours: tan, charcoal, orange), growing to a big cloud by f65 with hex debris chunks arcing out.
- f60-65: DESCENT into the pit; CAMERA TILTS DOWN/pans with him (horizon goes from y~330 to y~1280 in sheet = camera follows the fall, framing the hole as a cut-away). Body keeps the same crouched pose, legs straighten just before landing (f62-63: legs extend, then at f64-65 knees bend to absorb).
- f64-71: LANDING at the pit floor: knees bend deep (squash, ~f66-67), torso leans forward toward the pit wall, near hand touches the wall (f66: both hands forward; f67-71: one hand on the pit wall, small sand puffs at the wall/boots). Pose becomes a crouch pressed against the right wall, helmet down. Explosion cloud keeps growing outside (spheres don't move quickly: ~1-2 px/frame).
- RULES: (1) jump = 1 frame stretch + 2 frames up + hang 5-6 frames + fall 5 frames + squash 2 frames; (2) camera follows the character downward during the action (not static); (3) the character hugs the wall and touches it with a hand (contact with environment); (4) small sand puff particles at contact points; (5) explosion made of overlapped spheres (tan + charcoal) + orange core, long lingering.

## Frames 72-95 (c013-c016): CROUCH IN THE HOLE + camera drift
- f72-95: soldier holds a deep crouch (knees ~100 deg, torso leaning forward ~25 deg toward the right pit wall, ONE hand pressed flat on the wall at chest height, the other arm hanging/bracing slightly out). Pose is nearly frozen for 24 frames; only micro-motion: forearm tilts a few degrees (f78-95 hand slides slightly lower/outward), helmet nods ~2 px. Small sand particles trickle at the wall near the boots (f66-95).
- Explosion phase f72-84: tan+charcoal sphere clusters (6-10 spheres each) drift left/up and SHRINK/dissolve (spheres scale down and vanish from the edges f76-88), second explosion on the right ship at f72-79 (orange core + tan spheres, pops at the screen edge). Debris hex chunks fall slowly (floaty gravity, ~1 px/frame), fewer each frame. Ship smoke = grey stacked spheres stretching into a ribbon.
- CAMERA f72-95: continuous slow drift RIGHT + slight PUSH IN; the pit moves from x~270 to x~330 (sheet px) and the horizon goes from far-left ship to the big right ship (ship lines up beside the pit). Pit rim crest at left stays as a foreground silhouette. Parallax: ships move faster than the pit = camera orbit/truck, not pan.
- Eased start: at f84-86 the whole frame shifts ~60 px right in 3 frames (ease-in of the camera move), then a slow constant glide.
- LIGHT: pit interior shadowed on the left, warm lit on the right; the character has a soft dark contact shadow on the wall (cast shadow on the pit wall) = matters for the look.
- RULES: (1) after the big reaction the character settles into a HELD pose that reads clearly (crouch, hand on wall); (2) minor drift motions only; (3) the camera does the moving while the character holds; (4) dust trickle at contact points; (5) explosion spheres shrink out instead of fading.

## Frames 144-167 (c025-c028): SEAWATER FLOODS THE HOLE (camera behind him)
- Camera now 3/4 REAR: sees his back, helmet, sea + beach left, cliff right. Pit is a cut-away box; water = translucent teal box filling it (flat top with a lighter rim plate, a second translucent sheet at the top edge = surface).
- Water spray: ~80 WHITE STRETCHED ELLIPSOIDS (pill shapes, long axis = velocity direction) shooting UP from the pit, falling with gravity; f144 densest, thins out by f156-160, almost gone by f162. Each pill rotates to velocity. Very cheap, reads as splash.
- Character f144-167: rises to the water surface (only torso+helmet above water, legs visible through the translucent box = standing on the floor, water at chest). Both arms thrown UP and waving: f144-152 near arm straight up, far arm bent at elbow flailing near the helmet; f153-158 both hands clap/wave together above-left of the helmet (hands pressed together, "help" gesture), body bobs ~6 px; f159-161 arms fold down onto the helmet (cover pose again, hands on helmet, torso hunched, forearm blocks face); f162-164 one arm raised high and waving at the ship (hand open, fingers spread), other hand on helmet; f165-166 both arms stretch up and apart (diagonal V), f167 both arms straight up, palms open (surrender / "help me").
- Torso also turns: f144 body faces away-left, f158 turned towards camera ~30 deg, f162-167 turned right (toward beach).
- Helmet tilts: leans left f144-152, tips back f153-157 then forward f159-161 (nod with the hunch).
- No translation of the body (stays at the same x), only upper-body acting => the water level rises through him (he is "inside" the water volume, legs visible through it).
- Camera slowly settles (f144 -> f167 a ~40 px drift right/down), no cuts.
- RULES: (1) in a flood scene, show the character's legs through the translucent water; (2) several distinct arm poses in <1 s (up, clap, cover, wave, surrender) = every ~6 frames a new pose; (3) spray = stretched ellipsoids; (4) camera swings behind the character to show the threat approaching.

## Frames 168-191 (c029-c032): WALLS COLLAPSE INTO THE WATER
- f168-179: he keeps WAVING both arms up/open-palm, arms swap heights every ~3-4 frames (f168 left up/right up; f171 left higher; f174 left bent+right straight; f177 asymmetric V; f179 right straight, left bent). Torso is fixed, shoulders rock a few degrees, helmet turns slightly left/right (+/-10 deg) = "panic waving". Water surface stays calm and flat; legs visible below (stable standing).
- f170-173: first sand chunks (hex pebbles, 2-3 each side) appear on the pit rims and tumble down toward the water; f174-185 piles grow to 10-15 chunks each side, rolling/sliding down into the water; chunks that touch the water sink (small bubble spheres rise at the contact points f170-176 = translucent bubble spheres).
- f180-185: his near arm comes DOWN and hand goes to the helmet (f180-182: hand on helmet brim, other arm up) = "shield" gesture, then both arms up again f183-185 (left arm raised diagonal-left, right straight).
- f186-191: SINKING/WADING: arms spread wide (left arm reaches left, hand open at shoulder height f186-189; right arm up), torso slides lower into the water (shoulders now at water surface: body ~20 px lower per 5 frames), head stays up; he leans away and starts to tilt (torso twist) = swimming/treading start. Water box bottom shifts (box grows toward camera) f186+: pit walls widen as the camera moves down/forward (parallax: horizon ship stays, pit bottom moves up from y~700 to y~600 = camera is craning DOWN slowly while pushing in).
- Caption colours here: AND (cyan), THE WALLS (red), SLIDE IN (green): one accent colour per phrase, rotating.
- RULES: (1) character acts continuously even while the "story object" (collapse) is the focus: panic-waving with offset arms and head turns; (2) every ~3-4 frames a different arm pose, no still frames longer than 6; (3) debris pebbles rolling from rims + bubbles when they hit water; (4) he sinks gradually (body Y drops) as water rises/walls fall.

## Frames 192-215 (c033-c036): SLIDING IN / BURIED (walls cave, sand fills the pit)
- f192-203: He keeps panic-waving; he is now sinking: shoulders disappear below the water line (only head+arms visible at f195-203). Left arm reaches out and down toward the left rim (f198-203: forearm swings from raised to diagonal-down-left "grabbing"), right arm waves straight up (hand open, small wag +/-10 deg). Helmet tilts back-and-forth. Body is getting lower ~15 px per 6 frames (ease-in-out), NOT teleporting.
- Pit change: the pit walls slump inward (the V shape narrows, bottom becomes round "bowl" f198-215) = sand is sliding in and the water box shrinks (its height drops from 220 px to 100 px by f210). Pebbles on the rim shrink/disappear f192-200 (replaced by a continuous slope).
- Camera: slow dolly in (+scale ~1.5% per 6 frames) and crane down; horizon ship and cliffs drift left/up (parallax) = camera truck right + push in. Smooth, constant, no cuts. Pit centre stays at x~270.
- f204-209: left arm up and out (wave), right arm up; body at chest level shows only a sliver of the shirt; water surface now a flat lens.
- f210-215: the buried look: ONLY helmet + raised arms visible, then at f213-214 both arms raised in a V ("surrender" / drowning), at f215 arms come down forward and cross at the water surface (swimming/treading motion, hands low), body re-emerges a bit more (shirt visible), torso rotates slightly right.
- Caption colour sequence: SLIDE IN (green) -> ON TOP (cyan).
- RULES: (1) the character is pushed down gradually with sand/water level (no snap); (2) his arms keep moving even when the body is mostly hidden = "struggling"; (3) transition pose: V-arms (f213) -> arms down to tread (f215): ~2 frames; (4) as the pit slumps into a bowl the camera pushes in to keep him large.

## Frames 216-239 (c037-c040): CLIMBS OUT, STANDS, WALKS/RUNS AWAY
- f216-217: still waist-deep in the shrunken pit, torso now turned 3/4 toward the right (we see side+back), both arms out forward/left at hip-height, palms down (balance/wading pose, arms swing a little: hands 20 px apart per frame). Water is a thin lens at his waist.
- f218-221: STANDS UP out of the water: body rises ~60 px over 4 frames (ease-out), legs appear (grey shorts, knees, then boots at f221), torso rotates from side to FRONT/camera (f219-220 faces camera, arms hanging slightly away from body, palms back, "A-pose"), helmet level. f221 turns back to 3/4 (right side), left arm hangs back, right arm forward = pre-step.
- f222-226: IDLE SIDE-ON (profile facing left), upright, feet together in boots, arms hanging, a very small sway. HOLD ~5 frames. Camera static. Shadow on the pit wall to his right (long, cast to the right).
- f227-229: FIRST STEP: near arm swings forward, back leg lifts, torso leans slightly forward (~10 deg). Walk begins without a windup, accelerating.
- f228-239: WALK -> JOG cycle going LEFT-TO-RIGHT? NO: he moves from left (x~100) toward the right across the sheet (f228 x~90, f239 x~270 in sheet px = ~15 px/frame): so facing/moving to the RIGHT/camera-right. Gait: arms swing opposite to legs, knees high on the front leg (f232-233: stride very wide, back leg trailing with boot tip down), torso leans 10-15 deg forward, helmet steady, hands loose open. Cycle ~12 frames per stride (6 per step).
- Camera: stays static-ish through the walk, but the beach geometry changes: the shrunken pit's rim becomes a flat wall of sand (ground plane, wet dark beach sloping up to dunes with grass, grass tufts = thin cone planes). The character walks along the rim line (the cut plane) so we see him in profile.
- Caption: OF YOU (pink), SO (green), YOU MOVE (cyan, white first word).
- RULES: (1) get-up = rise + turn to camera + pause, then walk; (2) a hold of 4-5 frames BEFORE the first step (the viewer reads "he's okay"); (3) walk gait is a loose natural cycle with lean forward and arms swinging opposite; (4) character moves along the cut plane so the profile is visible.

## Frames 240-263 (c041-c044): RUN UP THE DUNE
- f240-251: JOG TOWARD the dune (screen right, slightly away from camera); gait cycle ~12 frames (left foot contact f240, f246, f252). Torso leaning forward ~15 deg, helmet bobs ~6 px vertical per step, arms swing opposite (near arm forward-down f240-243, back f246). His shadow on the dune is long and falls to the right.
- Beach changes: the wet dark flat beach on the left, a sand-coloured dune on the right with grass tufts (thin cone blades, 3 shades of green/olive), a thin tan slope. The ground line (cut plane edge) tilts up to the right: he climbs. Camera does a very slow push-in + tilt up (horizon/ship moves up out of the frame f240-263, dune grows).
- f252-263: slope climbing: stride becomes longer (f258-261: front leg stretched far forward, back leg trailing high), torso leans forward 20 deg, near arm swings forward/up, far arm back; helmet y rises relative to the ground (he goes uphill). Feet: boot soles visible at each contact, sand squish none. f263: he holds a long thin ROD/spade in his near hand (angled down behind) = a shovel/rifle appears at f263 (rotated stick at 45 deg).
- Small birds (seagull, white 3-4 px) glide at the top right throughout (f240-263), 1 px/frame drift: ambient life.
- Caption: UP BEHIND (white + red), THE DUNES (white + yellow): each 2-word chunk holds ~17-18 frames (0.6 s).
- RULES: (1) running gait: foot-flick (trailing boot tip up), vertical bob, forward lean, arm counter-swing; (2) as the terrain rises, lean increases and stride lengthens; (3) ambient birds; (4) camera pushes in slowly while character runs across the frame; (5) props (shovel/rifle) appear mid-action without a "pop".
