# Reference short: CHARACTER MOTION notes (frame by frame, 30 fps, 540x960 sheets, 6 frames each)
Why this file exists: the first pass (docs/ref3-frames-detail.md) described the soldier as "stands / crouches". The real animation is MUCH richer: he acts out every beat (digs with a scraping arm, stands up, ducks when the wave comes, is lifted by the water, treads water...). Our demo had a standing mannequin. Rule for our videos: the hero is ALWAYS doing something that matches the sentence, and reacts to every threat with a visible anticipation -> impact -> recovery.
Character: stylised 3D soldier (olive Brodie helmet, navy tee, grey shorts, brown belt, black boots), smooth shading, no face detail (helmet brim hides eyes), always seen in side/3-quarter profile when in the cut-away hole.

## Frames 96-143 (A FOXHOLE -> BUT THE -> FIRST WAVE -> FILLS IT)
- 96-101 DIG LOOP: half-crouch against the hole wall, knees bent ~45 deg, torso leaning forward into the wall, helmet down. The near arm scrapes the wall: hand touches the wall at 96, is pulled out at 97-98, back to the wall at 99, out at 100-101 = a ~3-frame scoop period (10 Hz), tiny sand puffs at the wall. Boots planted, body does not move otherwise.
- 102-104 STAND UP (3 frames, fast): knees straighten, torso rises, the arm swings back down behind his hip (102 hand at wall, 103 arm mid-air, 104 arm hanging). Ease-out, no overshoot.
- 104-113 IDLE LOOK (10 frames): standing straight, feet together on the hole floor, near arm hanging a little forward with a relaxed elbow, helmet slightly tipped UP at 105-107 (he looks at the incoming wave), then still. Only a 1-2 px sway. A wave builds on the horizon (rolling crest 102-107), a translucent teal wedge (the water volume) slides in from the left at 108-114 growing taller.
- 114-115 ANTICIPATION: the wave crest reaches the rim; he lifts the near hand toward the wall/chest (115) and his torso starts to turn away.
- 116-119 DUCK (4 frames): the wave mesh covers his head; he folds down - torso hunches forward, knees bend, helmet tips forward, arms come up over the head (118-119). White foam blobs (stretched white spheres) spawn on the crest.
- 120-125 BRACED HOLD (6 frames): crouched, back curved, helmet down, one hand sticks up ABOVE the water line and waves slowly (visible at 120-125 as a pale hand in the teal). Water cut line sits at his head height. Camera starts a slow crane UP + push in (hole gets ~1.4x bigger by 143, rim moves down the frame).
- 126-128 BLAST: white elongated spray particles (ellipsoids stretched along velocity) burst up from the hole (50+ of them), 126 first, 127-128 peak; the helmet pops up at 127, he unfolds.
- 129-134 PUSHED UP: head back looking up-left (129), both arms thrown UP into a V (130-131), body fully extended on tiptoe (131-134). The water in the hole starts to appear as a convex-topped translucent volume from the floor (131).
- 133-135 FLOAT-OFF: a boot lifts at 133, only toes touch at 134, feet leave the floor at 135 (he is now buoyant). Water level inside rises slowly 135-143 (curved top, darker blue side walls).
- 135-143 TREADING: helmet seen from behind (he turned his back to us), both arms flap alternately (one up and waving, the other reaching sideways to the wall), shoulders rock, chest-deep in the water; spray particles keep falling (150+ white spheres/ellipsoids in the air). Caption FILLS IT starts at 135.
- Take-away rules: (1) every threat gets ~3 frames of anticipation, ~3 frames of impact reaction, 6+ frames of held struggle; (2) water/foam = stretched white spheres, not a fluid sim; (3) the camera never cuts, it pushes and tilts during the reaction; (4) the hero is never idle for more than ~10 frames.

## Frames 0-47 (c001-c008)  [added out of order; read before 96-143]
- 0-2: soldier at right edge, profile facing left on wet sand, helmet brim hides face, arms hanging, near arm slightly forward. No dig yet.

- 24-41: pit finished (trapezoid cut-away, flat bottom). He stays head-down, near arm hanging forward with tiny sway (<2 px/frame). Sand pieces: tan hexagon chunks + round spheres, arcing up and out of the pit continuously, sizes mixed (big pale spheres at the lip, small dark chunks outward), gravity arc ~12 frames.
- f36-41: orange dot (ship hit) appears on the far ship at f38, white vertical streak (shell/tracer) rises above at f39-47 (sky). Soldier still head-down until f40.
- f40-41: REACTION START. Head comes up/turns toward the ship, arms lift forward to chest height (both hands out, palms down) at f41 = surprise/"what was that" gesture. Body leans back slightly.
- f42-47: ANTICIPATION -> COVER. Both arms fold up and wrap over the helmet (hands on top/back of head), elbows up, torso hunches and twists away from the ship (back toward camera), legs stay straight, feet planted. Pose reached in ~2 frames (f41 -> f43) = snappy, then held motionless (f43-47). Grey/black smoke puffs (sphere clusters) appear at his waist/back behind the near ship (f42-47).

- RULES: (1) reaction poses are reached in 2-3 frames then HELD still; (2) hold, don't wobble; (3) the reaction is triggered by an off-screen event (orange flash + white streak) 1-3 frames before the body moves; (4) pose before = relaxed, pose after = hands over head.

## Frames 48-71 (c009-c012): JUMP INTO THE HOLE
- f48-51: cover pose held (hands on helmet, back hunched). White spike (shell) grows bigger each frame while falling toward the ship (scale up = coming at camera). Orange flash dot on ship stays.
- f51-52: ANTICIPATION: the near arm/elbow lifts higher (hands rise ABOVE helmet), knees start to bend, body gets taller (stretch up) -> jump launch at f52.
- f52-53: LAUNCH. Feet leave the ground; boots point down, knees tuck forward (thighs horizontal-ish), hands stay raised over head in a "diver" pose. He rises ~1 body-width in 2 frames.
- f54-59: AIRBORNE hang, knees bent ~90 deg, torso upright, arms up over helmet, slightly leaning back; slowly drifting LEFT toward the pit (~5 px/frame in sheet scale) and DOWN. Shell explodes at f55-60 on the far side: orange fireball + tan/black sphere clusters (low-poly puffs, 3 colours: tan, charcoal, orange), growing to a big cloud by f65 with hex debris chunks arcing out.
- f60-65: DESCENT into the pit; CAMERA TILTS DOWN/pans with him (horizon goes from y~330 to y~1280 in sheet = camera follows the fall, framing the hole as a cut-away). Body keeps the same crouched pose, legs straighten just before landing (f62-63: legs extend, then at f64-65 knees bend to absorb).
- f64-71: LANDING at the pit floor: knees bend deep (squash, ~f66-67), torso leans forward toward the pit wall, near hand touches the wall (f66: both hands forward; f67-71: one hand on the pit wall, small sand puffs at the wall/boots). Pose becomes a crouch pressed against the right wall, helmet down. Explosion cloud keeps growing outside (spheres don't move quickly: ~1-2 px/frame).
- RULES: (1) jump = 1 frame stretch + 2 frames up + hang 5-6 frames + fall 5 frames + squash 2 frames; (2) camera follows the character downward during the action (not static); (3) the character hugs the wall and touches it with a hand (contact with environment); (4) small sand puff particles at contact points; (5) explosion made of overlapped spheres (tan + charcoal) + orange core, long lingering.

## Frames 72-95 (c013-c016): CROUCH IN THE HOLE + camera drift
- f72-95: soldier holds a deep crouch (knees ~100 deg, torso leaning forward ~25 deg toward the right pit wall, ONE hand pressed flat on the wall at chest height, the other arm hanging/bracing slightly out). Pose is nearly frozen for 24 frames; only micro-motion: forearm tilts a few degrees (f78-95 hand slides slightly lower/outward), helmet nods ~2 px. Small sand particles trickle at the wall near the boots (f66-95).
- Explosion phase f72-84: tan+charcoal sphere clusters (6-10 spheres each) drift left/up and SHRINK/dissolve (spheres scale down and vanish from the edges f76-88), second explosion on the right ship at f72-79 (orange core + tan spheres, pops at the screen edge). Debris hex chunks fall slowly (floaty gravity, ~1 px/frame), fewer each frame. Ship smoke = grey stacked spheres stretching into a ribbon.
- CAMERA f72-95: continuous slow drift RIGHT + slight PUSH IN; the pit moves from x~270 to x~330 (sheet px) and the horizon goes from far-left ship to the big right ship (ship lines up beside the pit). Pit rim crest at left stays as a foreground silhouette. Parallax: ships move faster than the pit = camera orbit/truck, not pan.
- Eased start: at f84-86 the whole frame shifts ~60 px right in 3 frames (ease-in of the camera move), then a slow constant glide.
- LIGHT: pit interior shadowed on the left, warm lit on the right; the character has a soft dark contact shadow on the wall (cast shadow on the pit wall) = matters for the look.
- RULES: (1) after the big reaction the character settles into a HELD pose that reads clearly (crouch, hand on wall); (2) minor drift motions only; (3) the camera does the moving while the character holds; (4) dust trickle at contact points; (5) explosion spheres shrink out instead of fading.
