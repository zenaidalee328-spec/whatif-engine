# Ref 3 frame-by-frame detail (bunker short, 30 fps, 1080x1920)
Viewed on 216x384 tiles, 15 frames per sheet. Notes are appended as the viewing goes. Frame numbers are 0-based.

## Look and style (corrected after the large view)
- NOT flat low-poly. The hero is a smooth, semi-realistic stylised 3D soldier: olive Brodie helmet, navy shirt, grey shorts, black boots, brown strap and belt. Blender-like soft lighting, sky with real cloud texture, sand with ripple texture and small pebbles.
- Ships: grey destroyer silhouettes (flat grey, 2-3 funnels, twin gun turret), 2-3 of them on the horizon.
- Debris and smoke are made of SPHERES: tan spheres plus black spheres for smoke, small brown spheres for flying sand.
- Muzzle flash = a flat orange circle on the ship. Shell = a thin white diamond streak. Impact = an orange burst plus a cluster of tan/black smoke spheres.

## Caption style (confirmed)
- Vertically centred on screen (y ~ 50%), horizontally centred. The text does not move with the camera.
- Rounded heavy sans (Poppins/Montserrat ExtraBold look), ALL CAPS, small (~3.5% of height), dark outline plus a soft shadow.
- 2-word chunks. The EARLIER word is white, the LATEST word is coloured. Colours seen: green, red, pink/magenta, yellow, cyan. They rotate and do not carry meaning.
- Chunks follow the voiceover: IF YOU 0-9 | DIG A 10-17 | HOLE ON 18-29 | A BEACH 30-42 | TO HIDE 43-54 | FROM WARSHIPS 55-77 | YOU HAVE 78-84 | A FOXHOLE 85-104 | BUT THE 105-112 | FIRST WAVE 113-...

## OVERALL CONCLUSIONS (for the engine)
1. Format: 1080x1920, 30 fps, ~62 s, one continuous "build" in a single persistent 3D diorama (beach + dune + bunker + tunnel + sea) with cut-away (cross-section) shots; the camera always flies THROUGH the diorama (no hard cuts except 2-3 whip pans).
2. Narrative formula: small thing (hole) -> "now add X" -> each step solves the problem that the previous step created (hole floods -> walls; enemy fire -> concrete; bullets -> tunnel; ships -> turn window sideways) -> comfort payoff (stove, cards, seagull gag) -> "congratulations, you have built a bunker". The funny small-gag beat near the end (seagull steals bread) is the re-engagement beat.
3. Captions: karaoke 2-word chunks, white earlier word + coloured current word (green, red, pink, yellow, cyan rotating), heavy rounded sans caps with a dark outline, centred vertically but anchored in the scene (moves with the camera framing between upper/lower third), 7-17 frames per chunk.
4. Visual language: stylised semi-realistic 3D (smooth shading, simple PBR, soft sky with texture clouds), cheap particles (spheres for dust, smoke spiral, flat orange explosion discs), thin laser/arrow overlays (pink sight-line, white/yellow arrows, translucent yellow cone), transparent "ghost" geometry for explanation.
5. Engine features needed: (a) karaoke caption style; (b) persistent diorama scene with timeline-driven props appearing (pop-in scale ease): hole, water, wall, bunker, stove, hedgehogs, mines, boats, tank, ships; (c) cut-away section mesh (clipping planes); (d) soldier rig with gun/pickaxe/cards/arms-up poses (hero.tsx has a suit man - needs new soldier + extra poses); (e) overlays: arrows, sight-line lasers, cone, double-arrow measure; (f) cheap explosions + tracers + dust; (g) camera path keyframes per shot (crane/push/whip).
